# Canon 0.15.0: the evidence chain holds over time

- **Branch:** `feat/0.15` (worktree `../canon-0.15`). Slices run in their own
  worktrees cut from `feat/0.15` and merge back in order. One PR to `main`.
- **Base:** `main` at `88cfcfd2` (0.14.0).
- **Version:** 0.15.0 (minor).
- **Status:** DONE (merged via PR #7).

## Why

Two inputs:

1. **The 2026-10-09 research briefing** (`docs/research/2026-10-09-canon-bard-weekly-briefing.md`,
   every item checked against its primary source). Its one-line conclusion is
   that success scores, approval records and independent reviews can all
   silently turn false unless context, execution and evaluator are bound
   together.
2. **Dogfood eval 2** (`~/Workspace/canon-dogfood/FINDINGS-2.md`), the 0.14
   acceptance rerun. canon now records the working loop, but several records
   go stale or vanish without anyone noticing:
   - A review keeps counting after its scenario and evidence change (G6).
   - Re-attesting a scenario quietly drops the earlier record's bound files
     from checking (G2).
   - Who approved the policy is recorded nowhere (G12).
   - A reviewer's failure is indistinguishable from a sign-off that never
     happened (briefing #8).

0.15 makes each record stay true after it is written, or say that it no
longer is.

From the briefing ranking (`BriefingMap015` scout, shortlist #1 and the
reviewer half of #3), 0.15 takes:

- **TestJack-style audited outcome** ([arXiv:2610.10619](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.10619)).
  canon records an external evaluator's re-audit and its counterexamples.
  canon does not generate tests.
- **The cross-provider review contract** ([arXiv:2610.10961](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.10961)).
  A reviewer's runtime, its inputs, and an explicit `indeterminate` outcome.

Deferred to 0.16, with reasons:

- **Skill-resolution and supply-chain provenance**
  ([arXiv:2610.11647](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.11647),
  [arXiv:2610.11169](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.11169)). It needs a Run-lineage
  design that dispatch consumers (Bard) must agree on.
- **Tool-operation policy results** ([GitHub changelog](https://github.blog/changelog/2026-10-07-local-sandboxing-for-github-copilot-now-generally-available/)).
  It needs an adapter contract with a runtime that produces them.
- **Spec-graph/code divergence** ([arXiv:2610.11725](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.11725)).
  Large, and needs an external analyzer.
- **ReviewBench-style scoreboard** (GitHub Blog). Follows the provenance work,
  so its numbers are grounded.

Out of scope permanently: running agents, sandboxing, live monitors (Cadence,
[arXiv:2610.12269](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.12269)). canon records the
results of those, never performs them.

## Decisions

- **D1 — A review is about what it saw (G6; briefing #8).**
  - `canon review add` pins the reviewed scenario's source digest and the ids
    and digests of that scenario's latest evidence records at review time. It
    computes them; the reviewer does not type them.
  - Under `require_review`, a pinned review qualifies only while the pins
    still match the scenario's current source digest and current latest
    evidence. Otherwise it is `unreviewed-promotion` with a "stale review"
    detail naming what changed.
  - Reviews written before 0.15 carry no pins. They keep qualifying and print
    one advisory line naming the count, never a violation. Canon's own ~320
    unpinned reviews are such legacy reviews.
- **D2 — Re-attesting never silently drops proof (G2).**
  - `evidence add` refuses (exit 2) a new latest record for a scenario if the
    previous latest record bound files that the new one does not bind.
  - The author must re-bind them, or pass `--drop-attachment <path>` (repeatable)
    together with `--drop-reason <text>`. The drop is recorded on the record.
  - The gate shows recorded drops in `canon status`.
- **D3 — An audited outcome is separate from the declared one (briefing #1, TestJack).**
  - A new evidence kind `audit` comes from `canon evidence add --kind audit`.
  - It carries `--evaluator-id`, `--evaluator-version` and `--evaluator-digest`,
    a result of `confirmed | refuted | indeterminate`, and optional
    counterexample reports (`--report`), which are bound and stored like any
    report.
  - It never replaces the declared test-run record. It sits beside it.
  - Policy: `spec_coverage.require_audit: { rate: <0..1>, seed: <string>, scope: [...] }`.
    Sampling is deterministic: a scenario is in the sample iff
    `sha256(seed + project_id + scenario_id)` falls below `rate`.
  - A sampled scenario without a non-indeterminate audit is
    `uncovered-cell (audit)`.
  - A `refuted` audit is a new failure class, `false-success`. The ship guard
    refuses it, and the latest declared `faithful` verdict does not override it.
  - `canon status` lists audit obligations.
  - canon never generates or runs the audit. An external evaluator does, and
    canon records it.
- **D4 — A reviewer's run can fail visibly (briefing #8).**
  - `review add` gains optional `--reviewer-provider`, `--reviewer-model`,
    `--reviewer-harness`, and `--outcome signed|indeterminate|unavailable`
    (default `signed`).
  - The input manifest digest is derived from the D1 pins plus `--pin`.
  - Only a `signed` review qualifies. `indeterminate` and `unavailable` are
    shown as gaps with their own detail, never as approval or rejection.
- **D5 — The policy has an approver (G12).**
  - `canon policy approve --by <who> [--note]` writes
    `.canon/policy.approval.json` with the policy digest, approver, time and
    note. The file is tracked.
  - `canon status` and `gate check` print an advisory when the policy is
    unapproved or changed since approval. It is not a violation.
  - `init`'s starter policy is unapproved until a human runs the command, and
    the skill's stop point names it.
- **D6 — Small integrity fixes.**
  - **Short SHAs (G8).** `finding add/close` resolve short SHAs through git,
    as `review add --pin` does.
  - **No writes to foreign repos (G14).** The access audit (`query`, `export`,
    `purge`) is written only beside the `canon.yaml` the command read, never
    into another repo or a directory without `canon.yaml`. *Amended at final
    review:* `status`, `context` and `gate check` never wrote the audit and
    still don't; auditing them would add a write to every CI gate run.
  - **Who records a defect (G5).** The skill tells the author to record
    defects they find themselves at the time they find them, before review.
  - **Task evidence (G13).** The skill says task evidence must bind the
    artifact or report that proves the task. `gate task` warns when a task's
    evidence has no attachment and its ref is a canon command.
- **D7 — Acceptance.**
  - A tamper suite in conformance: change a scenario after review, re-attest
    with fewer bindings, refute an audit, record an indeterminate reviewer.
    Each must turn the gate red or refuse, with the right class.
  - The 0.14 eval-2 repo copy (`nyan-omp-014`) must be re-checked with the
    0.15 binary, and every D1–D5 rule exercised on it.
