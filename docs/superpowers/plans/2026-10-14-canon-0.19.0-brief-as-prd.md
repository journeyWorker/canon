## Why

Dogfood eval 6 (canon 0.18.0 on omp, scope growth on a shipped subject; `docs/research/2026-10-14-dogfood-eval-6.md`) passed its acceptance criteria but showed the brief is the weak part of the loop:

- The brief template is two sections (`## Why`, `## What Changes`). The v2 brief mixed intent, an implementation decision (the art pipeline swap), the scenario plan and the questions in one section.
- `tasks.md` shipped 0/7 flipped (eval 5: 5 skipped). Checkbox state duplicates what scenarios already prove, and nothing reads it.
- Non-behavior work (the art pipeline swap) has no path of its own.
- The request was Korean; the final report came back in English.
- The `Approved:` line is read by presence only (`brief.rs:195`): a brief edited after approval stays approved.

Research behind the decisions (PR #55): the website's compared tools (`2026-10-14-compared-tools-research.md`), non-feature work (`2026-10-14-non-feature-work-research.md`), the agent-facing CLI report (`deep-research-report.md`), and impeccable's command/finding/gate contracts (public repo, clean-room).

## Decisions

- **D1: the brief format (#44).**
  - `canon change new` writes a PRD skeleton: Intent → Desired outcome → User flow → Step detail → Acceptance → Questions and assumptions → Out of scope → the approval comment.
  - Each section heading is preceded by a marker comment, `<!-- canon:section <key> role=<normative|context|question> -->`. Readers find sections by marker, never by heading text, so headings are in the brief's language (D6). Keys: `intent`, `outcome`, `flow`, `detail`, `acceptance`, `questions`, `out-of-scope`.
  - User flow is a numbered list; each item starts with a stable id `F1`, `F2`, … and reads "sees → does → becomes". Step-detail subheadings start with the same ids, in order.
  - Acceptance says, per step, what must be observable and which failure path is covered. Scenario ids are not written in the brief (they do not exist at brief time); scenarios point at steps (D4).
  - Briefs without markers (every brief before 0.19) are read as before; `brief check` reports them as `legacy` (advisory), never blocks.
- **D2: `canon brief check` and bare `canon brief` (#45).**
  - `canon brief check [--change <slug>] [--json]`, read-only. Findings carry a namespaced rule id, severity (`blocker|advisory`), section key, line, excerpt and hint. Exit 0 clean, 2 blocker findings, 1 operational error; stable string `error.code` on operational errors.
  - Blockers (structure): missing or out-of-order section; duplicate flow id; detail subheadings that do not match the flow ids in order; leftover placeholders (`TODO`, `TBD`, the template's own comment text); once the subject is `building` or later, a flow step no scenario points at, or a step with no `@case:failure` scenario.
  - Advisory (prose): closed per-language phrase lists (evaluative phrases, essay connectives, contrast rhetoric, colloquial endings for `ko`), sentence length, paragraph length, a normative section with no highlighted key sentence. Applied to `role=normative` sections only; elsewhere skipped. Thresholds and samples in the finding; never an authorship verdict.
  - Waivers: `<!-- canon:waive <rule-id> <section-key> "<reason>" -->` in the brief, one rule in one section, reason required. A waiver of a blocker is itself listed in the output. No global switch.
  - Bare `canon brief [--change <slug>]` prints the brief's state and the next one to three steps (`shape`, `clarify`, `harden`, `align`, `polish`, `approve`), derived from the findings. It never edits.
  - The ship guard runs the blocker rules for the subject's current change; `status` shows the finding counts.
- **D3: approval bound to the brief's bytes (#46).**
  - `canon brief approve --change <slug> --by <human> --note "<their words>"` writes `Approved: <who> <when> — "<words>" (brief sha256:<digest>)` into the brief and records the policy approval (one human answer approves both, as today). The digest covers the brief with every `Approved:` line removed. It refuses while `brief check` has blockers. This replaces the hand-written line plus `canon policy approve` in the skill (two calls become one).
  - Readers verify the digest. A brief changed after approval is not approved: status, `evidence add` and the ship guard say `BRIEF_CHANGED_SINCE_APPROVAL` and name the re-approval command. The human's words are still the only approval; re-approving needs a new human answer.
  - An `Approved:` line with no digest (pre-0.19) stays approved; status prints an advisory to re-approve with a digest.
- **D4: flow coverage replaces the checklist (#48).**
  - `canon scenario new --flow <F-id>` writes a `@flow:<change>.<F-id>` tag. The gate indexes it like `@subject`.
  - `canon status` lists each flow step of the subject's current change with its scenarios and their evidence/review state, and flags steps with no scenario or no failure case.
  - `canon change new` stops writing `tasks.md` for canon-owned changes. An existing `tasks.md` is still read; the skill calls it an optional work note that nothing gates on. `canon gate task` and `evidence add --task` stay for imported external plans.
- **D5: chore changes (#49).**
  - `canon change new --kind chore` writes a short brief: Intent, Category (`refactor|dependency|build|ci|infra`), Invariants (what must not change), Checks, Out of scope; the same markers. `brief check` has a chore rule set (no flow; invariants and checks required).
  - A chore adds no scenarios. Its evidence is the subject's suite run again at the chore commit, bound with `evidence add --report … --all-scenarios`; that supersedes the old evidence, which makes the pinned reviews stale (0.15), so an independent re-review follows. The ship guard is the normal one.
  - The brief is approved like any brief (one human stop). Work that changes behavior is not a chore; the skill sends it to a feature change.
- **D6: language (#50).**
  - `canon.yaml` gains `language: <tag>`; `canon init --language <tag>` writes it. Templates and prose rule lists ship for `en` and `ko`; another tag gets `en` headings and no prose rules (structure rules still apply).
  - The skill: briefs and the report to the human are written in `language`; unset, in the request's language, with "which language?" added to the brief's questions.
- **D7: skill (#47).** `canon/skills/reference/brief-{shape,clarify,harden,align,polish}.md`: scope, what to inspect, NEVER cases, verification, next step. Invariants in every pass: never invent policy (a change that could alter policy goes to Questions), explaining writes nothing, no pass requires another as a prerequisite, a pass closes only the findings it rechecked. Steps 1–2 of SKILL.md move to the PRD brief, `brief check`, and `brief approve`.
- **D8: trace-checked evals (#51).** `evals/dogfood/trace_check.py` reads omp/Codex session jsonl and reports: writes outside `.canon/` before the brief stop; writes during a question-only turn; reviewer session differs from the implementing one; canon calls, first-command validity (first call of each canon verb exits 0) and recovery depth (calls between a failed canon call and the next success). Unit tests on small recorded fixtures.
- **D9: website (#52).** Recheck the five cells listed in #52 against primary sources and update `strings.ts` (en/ko) and the footnote date.
- **D10: acceptance.**
  - (a) Eval 7 on omp, `language: ko`, a copy of `nyan-omp-019`. Turn 1: a new feature request. Pass: the brief passes `brief check` with no blockers before the stop; the report is Korean; nothing outside `.canon/` is written (D8 trace).
  - (b) Turn 2 approval. Pass: `brief approve` used; every flow step has a scenario and a failure case; shipped with gate clean.
  - (c) After shipping, edit the brief by hand; `evidence add` and the ship guard refuse with `BRIEF_CHANGED_SINCE_APPROVAL`.
  - (d) Turn 3: a chore request (dependency or build swap). Pass: `--kind chore` change, one human stop, no new scenarios, suite re-bound and re-reviewed, shipped.
  - (e) Not applicable: canon's own repo does not keep `.canon/changes/` for its releases (plans live in `docs/superpowers/plans/`), so there is no release change for `canon brief check` to read. The brief rules are covered by `crates/canon-cli/tests/brief_check.rs` and by (a)–(d).
