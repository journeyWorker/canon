# Canon 0.22.0: honest gates

- **Branch:** `feat/0.22` (worktree `~/external/canon/0.22`). Each slice gets its own worktree under `~/external/canon/` off `feat/0.22`, merged back in order. One PR to `main`.
- **Base:** `main` at 08b5677ef (0.21.1 + the Orca sandbox recipe).
- **Version:** 0.22.0 (minor).
- **Status:** IN RELEASE.
- **Issues:** #70, #71, #72, #73, #77, #78, plus the 0.21 deprecations due for removal.

## Why

The second dogfood round on 0.21.1 and the 2026-10-16 briefing found places where canon's gates reward the wrong move or say nothing:

- An honest `divergent` verdict on a known, upstream-tracked gap blocks shipping with `hint: null`; the only ways through are a waiver or a false `faithful` (#78).
- A chore reopens a shipped subject and the old evidence ships it at once: the chore's re-run is never asked for (#77).
- A blocker closed `fixed` lifts the block with no re-verification (#72).
- Weakening the approved `policy.yaml` passes the ship guard and `gate check`; the drift is advisory only (#71).
- `status` tells the planner to stop for the human while the brief is still an empty skeleton (#70).
- The reviewer reference never asks whether a test checks the scenario or just mirrors the implementation (#73).
- Three issues in a row (#62, #63, #78) were refusals with `hint: null`, which 0.20's contract was meant to end.

0.21 also promised to remove its deprecated names in 0.22.0.

## Decisions

- **D1: accepted divergence (#78).**
  - A scenario whose latest evidence is `divergent` can be accepted as a known gap: `canon divergence accept --scenario-id <id> --reason <text> --tracking <url-or-ref> [--expiry <date>] --reviewer <id> --role reviewer` writes a record that names the evidence it accepts (pinned like a review: new evidence for the scenario makes it stale). Reuse the existing divergence record kinds if one fits (check `divergence defer`); otherwise add one kind, documented.
  - The ship guard treats an accepted, unexpired, unstale divergent cell as covered. `status` and `gate check` keep listing it (`accepted-divergence <id> — <tracking>`), never silently. An expired acceptance blocks again.
  - Acceptance is a reviewer step (`roles: [reviewer]`), from a session that is not the evidence's.
  - The `uncovered-cell` refusal for a divergent verdict names both ways out in its hint: fix and re-attest, or `canon divergence accept`.
- **D2: re-verification after a fix and after a chore (#72, #77).** One rule, two triggers: a subject cannot ship while one of its scenarios with evidence has no evidence recorded after the trigger.
  - Trigger for #72: a blocker finding whose latest disposition is `fixed` stays blocking until every evidenced scenario of the change's subjects has evidence newer than the finding. New evidence already stales pinned reviews (0.15), so re-review follows.
  - Trigger for #77: a chore change approved on a subject requires evidence newer than the chore's approval for every evidenced scenario before `--to shipped`. `meta.next` after the reopen names `canon evidence add --all-scenarios …` then the re-review.
  - `change new` on a shipped subject prints the reopen command (`subject status <id> --to verifying --reason …`) in its output and `meta.next`.
  - The refusals name the scenarios and the batch command in `hint`.
- **D3: approved policy fails closed (#71).**
  - `policy.yaml` whose bytes differ from the approved digest (`Changed`), or whose approval cannot be read (`Unreadable`), is a violation: `gate check` exits 1, the ship guard and reopen refuse (`POLICY_CHANGED_SINCE_APPROVAL`), and `--override-reason` does not waive it. `Unapproved` (never approved) stays advisory.
  - `canon policy show [--json]`: path, current digest, approval state, approver, time.
- **D4: status follows the brief (#70).** A subject whose current change's brief has `brief check` blockers gets a planner step (`write the brief: canon brief check --change <slug> lists N blockers`, `role: planner`) instead of the human stop; the human stop appears only when the brief is clean and unapproved. `change new`'s `meta.next` follows the same rule.
- **D5: every refusal carries a hint.** A test fails when any `CliError::refused`/`failed` construction in canon-cli has no `.hint(…)` in its chain (static attribution like `tests/schema_codes.rs`), with an explicit, reasoned allowlist for errors whose message already is the next step. Fill every missing hint.
- **D6: reviewer checks the checks (#73).** `role-reviewer.md` gains a step: read each bound test against its scenario's Then steps; record a finding when a check pins behavior the scenario does not require or skips a Then step. Skill text only.
- **D7: remove the 0.21 deprecations.** `context-pack create`, `prompt register`, `divergence stage`, `demo attest`, and the two-positional `subject status <id> <state>` are removed; `deprecation.rs` and `meta.deprecated` go with them (clean cutover).
- **D8: acceptance.**
  - (a) Contract tests for D1–D5 and D7.
  - (b) Dogfood eval 10 on omp, orchestrated roles, on a copy of `nyan-omp-021`: a feature with one scenario honestly `divergent` against a stated external limitation (accepted with `divergence accept`), then a chore. Pass: shipped with the accepted gap still listed; the chore required a fresh suite run and re-review; no refusal with a null hint (trace).
